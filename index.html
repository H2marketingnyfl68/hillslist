Black Friday 2026 FAQ

When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday 2026 is Friday, November 27. Thanksgiving is Thursday, November 26, and Cyber Monday is November 30. Many retailers start their Black Friday sales a week or more early.

Should I buy now or wait for Black Friday?

It depends on the store. Every retailer on Hill's List gets my call: Hit means buy now, because I don't expect it to get cheaper. Split means buy some things now and wait on the rest. Hold means wait, because a better deal is likely coming.

Which Black Friday sales have already started?

Lots of them. Filter the deal floor by Hit to see the Black Friday sales I think are worth buying now, and check the date on each card to see when I last confirmed it.

How often is Hill's List updated?

Every day through Cyber Monday. I check retailer sites and sale emails, read the fine print, and update the offers and my calls.
